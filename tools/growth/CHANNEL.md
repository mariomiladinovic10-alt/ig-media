# YouTube channel optimisation: proposed copy (5 Nov 2026)
STATUS: PROPOSAL. Nothing here is applied. Mario approves each item before anything public changes.
Content performance comes first; metadata only helps people understand what the channel is.

## 1. Positioning (one line)
Short animated stories about what men learn about love, respect and themselves, told calmly.

## 2. About (description)
```
Quiet Man Laws is a channel of short animated stories about the moments that shape a man: the text he didn't send, the fight he didn't win, the friend who stopped calling, the day he chose himself.

Three series:
Emotional Truth: heartbreak, self respect and growing up emotionally.
Real Scenarios: real dating and relationship situations and what actually helps.
Psychology Reframes: common beliefs about confidence, love and discipline, looked at more honestly.

No hype, no "alpha" talk, no hate toward anyone. Just calm, useful stories.
New Quiet Law every day. Subscribe so the next one finds you.

Instagram: @quietmanlaws
```

## 3. Channel keywords
quiet man laws, mens psychology, relationship advice for men, self respect, emotional intelligence, dating advice, moving on, boundaries, discipline, stick figure animation, animated stories, quiet confidence

## 4. Playlists (one per series, added when each has 3 Shorts)
- Emotional Truth | Stories Every Man Recognises
- Real Scenarios | What To Do When It Actually Happens
- Psychology Reframes | What We Get Wrong About Love and Confidence
- Best Of Quiet Man Laws (top 10 by stayed to watch, refreshed monthly)

## 5. Shorts description template
```
{line 1: open loop that sets up the situation, never the ending} {1 emoji}

{one question that invites a personal story} Tell me in the comments.

{Series name}: a new Quiet Law every day. Subscribe so the next one shows up in your feed 🔔
Instagram: @quietmanlaws

#{topic1} #{topic2} #shorts #{topic3} #{topic4} #quietmanlaws
```
Hashtags: 5 to 7, first two are the strongest topic tags (they show above the title), then #shorts. Drop #stickfigure and #animation from YouTube (they describe the format, not the topic viewers search for).

## 6. Pinned comment template (pinned by Mario in the app; never auto)
A question that continues the story, written as the channel:
"Part of this story is true for a lot of us. What would you have done in his place? I read every reply."

## 7. Community posts (2 per week, once Community tab is available)
- Poll from the next Short's dilemma ("She says 'do whatever you want'. You: A ask what she really means B do what you want C go quiet").
- "Which story should get a part 2?" with the 3 best Shorts of the week.

## 8. Subscriber conversion
- Every YouTube version ends with "Subscribe for the next Quiet Law" (done from 5 Nov: endcta.py).
- Series tag on frame 0 and on covers so viewers see a set, not single clips.
- Part 2 only when a story earns it (comments asking): title "... (Part 2)", first frame shows "PART 2".
- Channel trailer is not used for Shorts channels; the Shorts shelf order is the storefront: keep the 3 best stories at the top by pinning them in a playlist.

## 9. Branding consistency
Profile picture: the white glow figure on black, same as the videos. Banner: "A quieter mind. A stronger man." in Inter ExtraBold with "STRONGER" in gold, one figure under a spotlight. Same handle everywhere: @quietmanlaws.
