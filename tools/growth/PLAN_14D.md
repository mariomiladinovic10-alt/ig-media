# 14 day growth experiment: 15 to 28 Nov 2026

One V3 Short per day at 22:00 Europe/Belgrade (16:00 New York), rotating Real Scenarios, Emotional Truth, Psychology Reframes. Silent narration (Mario's rule) with on screen lines, music2.py score, YouTube end line "Subscribe for the next Quiet Law". Production per tools/growth/STYLE_GUIDE.md and tools/animate/SCRIPT_ENGINE.md section 9.

Approval gates: (1) Mario approves scripts below, (2) OpenArt credits (about 150 to 160 per Short; account has 7 now), (3) Mario approves finished videos before scheduling. Nothing is published automatically by this plan.

Metrics for every Short at 24 h and 72 h: public views, engaged views, stayed to watch vs swiped away, average view duration (seconds), average % viewed, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained (python3 tools/analytics/analyze.py --report VIDEO_ID). Compare with the channel's own story median, never with fixed promises.
